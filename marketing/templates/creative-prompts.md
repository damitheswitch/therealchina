# Reusable creative prompts

Draft templates. Replace bracketed fields before use. A tool outside the
repository cannot read local paths unless you attach their contents/assets.
Use the approved brief and evidence, not model memory, for factual claims.

## Copy and concept

```text
Prepare a draft for The Real China using the supplied brand guide and brief.

Audience: [one segment]
Objective: [one outcome]
Approved angle: [angle]
Placement and language: [placement, language]
Core message: [one message]
Supported facts: [claim IDs, wording, sources, limits]
CTA and destination: [approved CTA, tested destination]
Commercial relationship and disclosure: [none, or approved wording]

Return:
1. Hook.
2. Main copy or script.
3. Exact on-screen text.
4. Caption and CTA.
5. Visual concept.
6. Evidence gaps and questions.

Keep the writing specific and natural. No em/en dashes, invented reviews,
student quotes, counts, guarantees, scarcity, or unsupported superlatives.
Do not present this draft as approved or publish it.
```

## Image or video keyframe

```text
Create illustrative visual material for [campaign ID / asset ID].

Purpose in the campaign: [purpose]
Subject and action: [subject, appearance, action]
Setting: [setting; distinguish illustrative from an actual documented location]
Composition: [framing, focal point, negative space for later text]
Lighting and mood: [specific direction]
Palette: [approved brand palette and how it is used]
Reference assets attached: [IDs, and what each reference controls]
Continuity requirements: [subject, clothing, objects, setting]
Output shape: [confirmed dimensions/aspect ratio]
Keep clear: [confirmed crop and caption/disclosure zones]

Do not generate the TRC logo, typography, watermarks, review cards, official
documents, university signage, or testimonial quotes. These will be added from
approved sources during editing. Do not imply this scene is documentary proof.
```

## Animation from an approved keyframe

```text
Animate the attached approved keyframe [reference ID] for shot [shot ID].

Duration: [seconds]
Narrative purpose: [what the viewer needs to understand]
Subject movement: [one clear action]
Camera movement: [direction, speed, framing]
Environment movement: [what moves, or stays still]
Preserve: [identity, wardrobe, composition, palette, object positions]
Start and end states: [states and transition into the next shot]
Caption/disclosure zones to keep clear: [zones]
Audio: [none, or separately approved direction]

Avoid identity drift, invented text/logos, object morphing, flashing, and
unmotivated cuts. Exact captions and branding will be added during editing.
The result needs full playback review, not just first-frame approval.
```

## Video assembly handoff

```text
Assemble only the supplied approved shots and sourced material.

Shot order and timings: [shot IDs, durations]
Voiceover: [approved script and licensed/consented voice source]
On-screen text and captions: [exact copy and timings]
Logo and type: [approved master and fonts]
Music/footage rights references: [references]
CTA/end card: [exact text, destination]
Commercial/synthetic-media disclosures: [wording, placement, duration]
Export requirements: [confirmed placement requirements]

Keep editable sources. Export a new version without overwriting approvals.
Return output locations and anything not verified. Do not publish.
```

Record the actual prompt and settings in `production-record.json`, including
manual edits. Templates are portable instructions, not promises that every
model supports each control or will reproduce the same output.
