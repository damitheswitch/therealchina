## Strategy and copy

- [ ] One primary audience, objective, message, and CTA.
- [ ] Copy is specific, human, and consistent with the brand guide.
- [ ] No em/en dashes, fake urgency, invented testimonials, or unsupported hype.
- [ ] Destination matches the promise and has been tested on the intended device/network.
- [ ] Language has a qualified review where needed.

## Evidence and trust

- [ ] Claims map to dated evidence; limits remain visible.
- [ ] No claim that all reviewers/enrollment are verified.
- [ ] Counts, prices, product performance, and screenshots are current and genuine.
- [ ] No demo data, fabricated student quotes, or generated campus imagery presented as real.
- [ ] Review requests accept honest positive and negative experiences without rating pressure.
- [ ] No unapproved rewards, badges, or admissions promises.
- [ ] A VPN reward does not imply unlimited service; the 2GB allowance and eligibility are easy to see near the offer.
- [ ] Anonymity copy describes public masking, not guaranteed untraceability; account controls match the actual journey.
- [ ] Reward-delivery email is distinguished from optional review contact and provider-marketing consent.
- [ ] Commercial relationship and disclosure are accurate and visible in each placement.
- [ ] Partner review and public policy alignment are complete for commercial content.

## Assets and accessibility

- [ ] Logo is from the approved master, not generated.
- [ ] Fonts, imagery, music, faces, voices, quotes, and footage have appropriate rights.
- [ ] Actual exports were checked for broken text, anatomy, logos, signs, and fake UI.
- [ ] Video was watched fully for continuity, flicker, captions, timing, and audio.
- [ ] Text/disclosures stay readable on a phone and clear of placement controls.
- [ ] Contrast is sufficient, speech is captioned, and the message works without sound.
- [ ] Alt text or a useful text description is prepared where supported.
- [ ] Any synthetic-media disclosure decision is recorded and requirements checked.
- [ ] No personal data, private documents, or identifying details are exposed.

## Publication and measurement

- [ ] Correct dimensions, duration, format, and current placement requirements checked.
- [ ] Final caption, link, disclosure, and asset version are reviewed together.
- [ ] Community/admin permission is confirmed for group distribution.
- [ ] Primary outcome and attribution limits are documented without sensitive tracking.
- [ ] Actual generation costs and approved spend limits are recorded where applicable.
- [ ] Owner approval is recorded for these exact versions and placements.
- [ ] Publishing and any spending have separate explicit authorization.
