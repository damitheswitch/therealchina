## Later operational decisions

- Exact meaningful-detail/reward criterion. Do not blindly count all form fields
  or confuse the existing detailed-review label with the proposed 90% threshold.
- VPN redemption, allowance expiry, capacity, fulfillment email handling, support,
  duplicate prevention, and any profile-related discount.
- Provider vetting, evidence for performance/security, and financial relationship.
- Translation-office agreement or a separately scoped coordination service.
- Complaint verification, response, refund responsibility, and escalation.
- Generation budget, first visual references, asset rights, storage, and metrics.
- Actual anonymous submission/account journeys and optional WeChat visibility.

For rights: the owner's earlier assumption that anything publicly posted can be
reused is not a permission record. Check appropriate rights for each intended use.
For privacy: the owner's intent to protect students is not evidence that review
content cannot identify them.

## How answers become documentation

commercial policy for incentives/services, claims register for evidence, and
