# Commercial content and trust

Status: owner direction recorded 2026-10-27; operational details remain draft.
This is an internal decision framework, not public terms or legal advice.
It does not establish any partner relationship.

## Current direction and unresolved conflict

The owner rejects university/agency placements and paid influence over reviews.
Vetted services should answer actual student requests, not steer students toward
whoever pays. Useful reviews and student needs come before providers or income.
Revenue hypotheses are untested; neither newcomers nor current students are
assumed to be the only paying audience.

**Owner clarification:** "No sponsorships or partner placements" is intended to
exclude paid steering toward universities/agencies and paid influence on reviews,
not vetted student-requested services.

**Public-copy alignment still required:** `frontend/src/lib/trustContent.ts`
currently uses the broader wording "No sponsorships, no partner placements, no
reviews for sale." Record the owner's narrower meaning, but do not assume readers
will infer it. Before service promotion, align the public wording and actual
practice. This kit does not edit public policy or authorize a launch.

Student-first and editorially independent are the intended identity. Do not claim
that a financial relationship does not exist because helping students is the
primary motive. Disclose actual payments, commissions, and relevant provider
relationships; do not imply an audited company is supplying a friend-run service.

## Proposed trust boundaries

1. Payment must not change university ratings, review order presented as
   independent merit, moderation, or the handling of negative reviews.
2. Keep editorial recommendations and commercial placements distinguishable.
   Do not sell an undisclosed recommendation or call a sponsored list independent.
3. Disclose a material relationship clearly near the recommendation/link and in
   the relevant video/caption. Do not bury it only in a profile or policy page.
4. Publish a recommendation only if there is a user-centered reason supported
   by evidence. Commission size is not the review criterion.
5. Do not reuse review-form contacts for product marketing or upload them to ad
   audiences. A new consent/privacy decision and authorized implementation are
   separate work, not implied by collecting reviews.
6. Share leads with a partner only after explicit, informed permission for that
   specific handoff. Do not put personal leads or documents in campaign files.
7. Do not ask contributors for favorable reviews, condition benefits on ratings,
   or advertise unapproved incentives.
8. Have a way to correct, pause, or withdraw a recommendation when evidence,
   partner behavior, or service quality changes.

Suspected fake/commercial reviews should be investigated under the existing
moderation policy, with removal when that policy is breached. The owner wants
to contact authors to check concerns where contact is available and authorized.
Do not expose anonymous identities or treat harsh criticism as evidence of fraud.

## Student support and complaints

Owner direction: invite students to contact TRC with questions, requests for help,
or service complaints, not only sales inquiries. Use the contact source in
`frontend/src/lib/seo/site.ts`.

Help pursue resolution or a refund. A verified unresolved complaint is sufficient
to remove the provider recommendation; do not keep it up because it earns money.
Recommend a replacement only after new vetting. Actions may be combined or
escalated depending on the situation.

The verification process, response time, provider agreements, refund responsibility,
and limits are not yet defined. Do not promise automatic TRC-funded reimbursement,
a lawsuit on every student's behalf, guaranteed outcomes, or that no recommended
service can ever fail. The owner's clarified direction is case-dependent support
and provider removal, not an unconditional legal/financial guarantee.

Disclosure is necessary but does not, by itself, resolve a conflict of interest.
The owner may decide some revenue models are not worth offering.

## Partner decision card

Complete this privately for each prospective partner. Keep only a non-sensitive
reference and campaign approval status in this repository.

- Partner identity, business location, accountable contact, and role.
- Customer need, audience, service, and regions served.
- Actual testing or due-diligence evidence, limitations, and check date.
- Credentials where relevant, with independent checks rather than a logo alone.
- Commission basis, payment timing, incentives, and any exclusivity.
- Customer price, fees, refund/cancellation terms, and who provides support.
- Complaints, failure cases, escalation owner, and stop conditions.
- Lead/data flow, consent, retention, and whether any documents are required.
- Rights to use the name, logo, discount, footage, or claims.
- Applicable advertising/platform requirements and qualified review where needed.
- Exact disclosure, approved placements, owner decision, and review date.

Unknown answers mean the offer is not ready. No legal conclusion, certification,
or partner endorsement should be inferred from this checklist.

## Product-specific boundaries

### VPNs

Future reward direction: one 2GB allowance for a full or approximately
90%-complete review; images are optional. Sentiment and public identity do not
affect eligibility. Email is needed for delivery. The exact detail standard,
frequency, expiry, capacity, delivery process, and relationship with the friend
are undecided. Profile completion alone is not confirmed as an eligible action;
the previously mentioned profile-linked discount still needs definition.

Lead with the useful reward and the student-helping mission together. The
headline need not contain the number, but the 2GB limit and eligibility must be
easy to see near the offer and before submission. Do not imply unlimited service
or hide material limits behind a generic "free VPN" promise.

Receiving a reward does not automatically consent someone to provider marketing,
contact sharing, or future sales. Define fulfillment and optional future purchase
separately; offer redemption should not silently become a lead-list transfer.
Document the incentive relationship and check distribution/review-disclosure
requirements before launch.

Record product/version and the dates and conditions of testing. Performance can
change by network and time. Do not promise universal availability, guaranteed
access in China, guaranteed privacy/security, or legality everywhere. Check
current product terms, placement restrictions, and relevant requirements before
promotion. Decide whether TRC is the seller or a referrer; do not obscure who
handles payment, refunds, or support.

### Translation

If the office does not agree to a referral arrangement, the owner may explore
coordinating requests and paying the office on students' behalf. This would
require a separate decision about responsibilities, pricing, consent, document
handling, and recourse; it is not an established workaround or approved service.

Distinguish general translation from certified/notarized work or a receiving
institution's requirements. Do not promise official acceptance without checking
the exact requirement. Sensitive documents must not pass through marketing AI
tools or this repository.

### Admissions

Remain on hold until the owner decides whether this fits TRC. If considered,
evaluate whether the advice stays useful when the best-fit university pays
nothing, how alternatives are shown, who makes recommendations, and how a
student can complain. A separate label or brand alone does not remove the
underlying conflict. No outcome guarantees or invented school relationships.

## Disclosure drafts, use only when factually correct and approved

- Affiliate link: "We may earn a commission if you buy through this link."
- Paid collaboration: "Paid partnership with [partner]."
- Referral: "If you choose this service through TRC, the provider may pay us a referral fee."

Replace placeholders and check accuracy for the actual arrangement and channel.
Do not add "at no extra cost" unless the pricing terms support it.

## Launch gate

No commercial campaign proceeds until the partner/offer, evidence, public policy
alignment, disclosure, destination, privacy handling, and exact asset version
are approved. Budget approval is separate. Start small with a review date and a
documented reason to pause, not an assumed permanent endorsement.
