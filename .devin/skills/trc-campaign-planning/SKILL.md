# TRC campaign planning

All paths below are relative to the repository root, not this skill directory.
Follow `AGENTS.md`. Read `marketing/README.md`, the three files in
`marketing/brand/`, and the selected campaign brief before planning.

## Procedure

1. Identify the user's campaign and inspect its brief. If none exists, use
   `marketing/templates/campaign-brief.json`. Do not overwrite another campaign.
2. Contributors are TRC's permanent first priority: current students and alumni
   wherever they live. Use role/need, not geography, for audience selection.
   Choose one objective, CTA, and outcome. Keep contribution campaigns separate
   from standalone service sales. The future review thank-you may be featured
   only once its offer, limits, fulfillment, and launch are approved.
   A commercial objective must specify the buyer, not assume all students buy.
3. Separate owner decisions, repository evidence, hypotheses, and unknowns.
   Ask two or three related questions interactively; read
   `marketing/review-questions.md` first to avoid repeating settled decisions.
   Update understanding from each answer. Leave unrelated gaps visible instead
   of replacing the conversation with another long questionnaire.
4. Inspect relevant current product/content sources before promising features.
   Use `marketing/brand/claims-register.md` and add campaign-specific evidence.
   Never turn a development target or owner report into verified live evidence.
5. For commercial work, apply `marketing/brand/commercial-policy.md`.
   Flag the current public-policy conflict. Admissions remains on hold.
   A blocked commercial concept may be explored as an internal draft only.
6. Propose three meaningfully different angles. For each, give the audience
   insight, hook, proof needed, CTA, suitable format, and one testable hypothesis.
   Recommend one based on usefulness and available evidence, not assumed virality.
7. Keep deliverables small and adapted to chosen channels. Confirm placement
   requirements rather than inventing current platform rules. No channel,
   language, cadence, conversion target, or budget is approved by default.
8. Define the primary metric, source, quality guardrails, and attribution limits.
   Do not equate views with useful visits, starts with contributions, or
   affiliate clicks with sales. No invented baseline or tracking implementation.
9. Save an updated draft brief only when file edits are requested/authorized.
   Preserve pending approvals. Stop for owner angle approval before paid generation.

## Handoff

Return the recommended angle, two alternatives, the brief location, evidence
gaps, and the few decisions needed next. Asset production follows
`.devin/skills/trc-asset-production/SKILL.md`.

Do not publish, spend, contact partners, send messages, upload audience data,
change public policies, or launch subagents without explicit authorization.
